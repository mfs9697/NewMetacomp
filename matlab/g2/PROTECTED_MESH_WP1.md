## WP1: full protected mesh

### Construction (new files; nothing existing was edited)

- **`g2_protected_exterior_mesh.m`:** a new native PDE exterior with the same controls as the qualified three-hole candidate:
  - Hmax 6 mm, Hgrad 1.12, Hmin 0.12 mm;
  - lip Hedge 0.45 mm, pencil half-width 0.020 mm;
  - three true holes (R = 6.35 mm) plus three **nonphysical** 3R = 19.05 mm guide faces with Hface 1.5 mm.

  The channel ends at a **64-gon rim cavity** whose vertices are the collar's analytic outer nodes; the crack-entry vertex is split on x = c_x ∓ w. Rim edges carry Hedge = chord (0.3238 mm), and the run **holds** unless the rim carries exactly 65 node identities, i.e. 64 single segments. The lips are collapsed onto x = c_x without welding.
- **`g2_join_protected_mesh.m`:** joins the unchanged p010 block (regenerated by the PR #19 code) to that exterior. The 65 rim identities are replaced by the collar's 65 outer identities: left entry → `outer(33)`, right entry → `outer(65)`. Block rows 1–2,916 and their nodes keep the block numbering. T6 comes from the pinned `T3toT6_fast`, and all gates are recomputed.
- **`g2_protected_tip_checker.m`:** an independent audit of the **saved** arrays. It does not call the mesher, the join or the p010 builders. It checks:
  - kernel and collar identical to the P0 evidence MAT;
  - single tip, six-triangle tip fan, first edge 0.041667 mm;
  - exactly 2,916 elements inside r = 3.3 mm, no foreign node inside;
  - both 64-edge seams with two owners on opposite sides; rim edges are single 1/64 chords;
  - two node identities per protected crack-face position;
  - FE-nodal q recomputed independently, 2,443 active parents in the kernel, q = 0 on collar and exterior;
  - clearances; neighbour ratio near the rim.
- **Runners:**
  - `run_g2_protected_mesh.ps1` checks the source pins and the RAM guard, then runs three separate MATLAB processes: P0 → mesh → audit;
  - `run_g2_protected_mesh_batch.m`, `run_g2_protected_audit_batch.m`;
  - negative controls: `check_g2_protected_tip_checker_controls.m`.
